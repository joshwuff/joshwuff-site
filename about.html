Meet the dawg

My two sonas! :D

I never thought I would have more than one sona, but here we are! Here's a little snippet of who both of them are and how they came to be! :3

joshwuff

Wockle / First Sona

joshwuff is my first sona, a silly little 2 foot 3 inch puppydog. His official species is a "wockle", a wolf jackal hybrid. There is a ton of lore behind this that I may delve into one day, but that is for another time in another place. He started off as a recolor in a free ych coloring book page I found yeaaaars ago. I have been modifying his look to match my personality and interests for years now.

Peachee

Peanut butter cheese dog / Second Sona

Peachee is my second sona! I never thought that I would have another sona, but after being inspired by someone's "food-sona" on twitter one day, I decided to take a little spin of my own and ended up making Peachee! He is created from my love of peanut butter and cheese, specifially, the most unhealthy version of peanut butter, the Reese's peanut butter jar. The ongoing joke I have with this sona is that if I eat too much peanut butter and cheese, joshwuff just turns into Peachee. x3
